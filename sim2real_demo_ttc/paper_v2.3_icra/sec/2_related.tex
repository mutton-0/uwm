\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Open-loop benchmarks and their discontents.}
nuScenes~\cite{caesar2020nuscenes} and the nuPlan-derived NAVSIM
protocol~\cite{dauner2024navsim} score a policy by comparing its plan against logged
human behaviour, the latter through a non-reactive simulation with map-based
compliance terms. Both are displacement- or compliance-centric: a policy that never
reacts to a hazard can score well as long as its trajectory stays close to the
recorded one and inside the drivable area. Our Faithfulness axis is designed to be
insensitive to exactly the thing these scores reward, and we quantify the disagreement
directly rather than asserting it.

\paragraph{Causal confusion in imitation learning.}
Policies trained by behaviour cloning routinely latch onto correlates of the expert's
action rather than its causes~\cite{dehaan2019causalconfusion}. The standard remedy is
intervention at the input; the standard difficulty is constructing an intervention
that removes the cause without also removing everything correlated with it. Our
three-arm same-frame design, with an equal-area control patch and (for LiDAR
consumers) a mirrored-box point deletion, is our answer to that difficulty.

\paragraph{Probing and control tasks.}
Reading structure out of frozen representations is only meaningful against a control
that shares the probe's capacity~\cite{hewitt2019designing}. We adopt the
random-initialisation control and additionally report a position-only floor, which in
our setting accounts for the majority of the raw probe score.

\paragraph{Localisation by activation patching.}
Replacing an activation with its counterpart from a paired input localises which
component carries a behavioural difference. The construction is only interpretable
when the patched set is a sufficient cut for that pairing; we enforce this with an
explicit patch-all self-check, which in our experiments rejected one policy--corpus
cell outright and forced a correction to the intervention itself.

\paragraph{Segmentation supervision without labels.}
We use class-agnostic masks from SAM~\cite{kirillov2023sam} as an objective,
task-independent probe target, avoiding any human judgement about which objects count
as hazards.
